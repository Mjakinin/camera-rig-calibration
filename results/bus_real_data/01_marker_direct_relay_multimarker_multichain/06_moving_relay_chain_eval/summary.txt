Moving relay multichain aggregation
================================================================================

Candidate generation:
  All valid combinations are evaluated:
  root_marker × root_moving_frame × target_marker × target_moving_frame
  A candidate is valid if both moving frames are registered in COLMAP and all PnP observations exist.

Outlier rule, no GT:
  Compute robust consensus from all candidates.
  Candidate is inlier if translation and rotation deviation are <= median + 3*MAD.
  Floors: 0.30 m and 7 deg.

Final estimate:
  weighted mean of MAD inlier transforms, weighted by observable marker quality.
  GT is used only after aggregation for evaluation.
